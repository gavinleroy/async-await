\section{Design dimensions}\label{sec:designdims}

Language designers must decide how the async/await syntax will expand into the set of asynchronous building blocks. The expansion is often influenced by patterns in the pre-existing base language as well as performance and memory expectations of the community.

In this section we provide the async/await design dimensions. The design dimensions map the async/await syntax to the runtime behavior of the desugared program. The design dimensions are: \textit{eagnerness,} \textit{extent,} \textit{reference strength,} \textit{destruction,} \textit{propagation,} \textit{suspension,} and \textit{cancellation.}

\begin{table}[H]
  \centering
  \begin{tabular}{ll}
    \toprule
    \Eagerness   &%
      \footnotesize%
      \begin{tricolumn}
        \textsc{\lazy} & \textsc{\seager} & \textsc{\eager} \\
        \begin{pros}
          \item Predictable allocation
          \item Predictable execution
          \item User-space implementable runtime
        \end{pros}  & 
        \begin{pros}
          \item Intuitive
          \item Protect current thread
          \item Maximize multi-core \abbr{cpu} usage
        \end{pros}  &
        \begin{pros}
          \item Intuitive
          \item May avoid \task allocation
        \end{pros}  \\
        \begin{tabular}[t]{@{}ll@{}}
          JavaScript & \CSharp
        \end{tabular} &
        \begin{tabular}[t]{@{}ll@{}}
          Swift & Smol
        \end{tabular} &
        \begin{tabular}[t]{@{}lll@{}}
          Tokio & Asyncio & Trio
        \end{tabular}
      \end{tricolumn} \\

    \midrule
    \Extent &
      \footnotesize%
      \begin{bicolumn}
        \textsc{\dyn} & \textsc{\indef} \\
        \begin{pros}
          \item Prevent resource leaks
          \item Resemble synchronous execution
        \end{pros}  &
        \begin{pros}
          \item Flexible
          \item Intuitive to developers (akin to GC)
        \end{pros}  \\
        \begin{tabular}[t]{@{}llll@{}}
          JavaScript & \CSharp & Tokio & Smol
        \end{tabular} &
        \begin{tabular}[t]{@{}ll@{}}
          Swift & Trio
        \end{tabular}
      \end{bicolumn} \\

    \midrule
    \References  &
      \footnotesize%
      \begin{bicolumn}
        \textsc{\strong} & \textsc{\weak} \\
        \begin{pros}
          \item Deterministic destruction
          \item \Tasks run to completion (intuitive)
        \end{pros}  &
        \begin{pros}
          \item Prevent resource leaks
        \end{pros}  \\
        \begin{tabular}[t]{@{}lll@{}}
          JavaScript & \CSharp & Tokio
        \end{tabular} &
        \begin{tabular}[t]{@{}ll@{}}
          Asyncio & Smol
        \end{tabular}
      \end{bicolumn} \\

    \midrule
    \Destruction &
      \footnotesize%
      \begin{tricolumn}
        \textsc{\awaited} & \textsc{\cancelled} & \textsc{\terminated} \\
        \begin{pros}
          \item Prioritize data integrity
          \item Usable by systems with no built-in cancellation
        \end{pros}  &
        \begin{pros}
          \item Prioritize data integrity
        \end{pros}  &
        \begin{pros}
          \item Prioritize responsiveness
        \end{pros}  \\
        \begin{tabular}[t]{@{}lll@{}}
          JavaScript & Tokio
        \end{tabular} &
        \begin{tabular}[t]{@{}ll@{}}
          Swift & Tokio & Smol & Asyncio
        \end{tabular} &
        \begin{tabular}[t]{@{}l@{}}
          \CSharp
        \end{tabular}
      \end{tricolumn} \\


    \midrule
    \Propagation &
      \footnotesize%
      \begin{bicolumn}
        \textsc{\destruction} & \textsc{\never} \\
        \begin{pros}
          \item Prioritize data integrity
          \item Usable by systems with no built-in cancellation
        \end{pros}  &
        \begin{pros}
          \item Prioritize responsiveness
        \end{pros}  \\
        \begin{tabular}[t]{@{}lll@{}}
          JavaScript & Asyncio & Trio
        \end{tabular} &
        \begin{tabular}[t]{@{}ll@{}}
          \CSharp & Swift
        \end{tabular}
      \end{bicolumn} \\


    \midrule
    \Suspension  &
      \footnotesize%
      \begin{bicolumn}
        \textsc{\static} & \textsc{\dynamic} \\
        \begin{pros}
          \item Reduce starvation
        \end{pros}  &
        \begin{pros}
          \item Reduce overhead
        \end{pros}  \\
        \begin{tabular}[t]{@{}l@{}}
          JavaScript
        \end{tabular} &
        \begin{tabular}[t]{@{}llll@{}}
          \CSharp & Swift   & Asyncio & Trio    & Tokio   & Smol
        \end{tabular}
      \end{bicolumn} \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabular}
\end{table}
