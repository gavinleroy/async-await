\section{Discussion}\label{sec:discussion}

The book is not closed on the design of straight-line asynchrony. Communities are actively working on the usability and safety of asynchronous systems. For usability many are concerned about the function coloring problem first introduced by \citet{nystrom_what_2015}, and others are designing increasingly sophisticated type systems to statically check the safety of asynchronous program.
